You and I have been discussing a feature.  
Your job is to convert that conversation into a formal development plan.

1️⃣ Interpret the feature ONLY from the conversation context.  
If anything is unclear, ask one concise clarification question.

2️⃣ Create (or update) the feature plan file:
   File: docs/FEATURE_PLAN.md

The plan must include:

SECTION 1 — Feature Summary
- What the feature is
- Who benefits and why
- Success criteria

SECTION 2 — Scope & Assumptions
- In scope / Out of scope
- Dependencies
- Key constraints

SECTION 3 — Architecture & Design
- High-level design
- Data and control flow
- Risks or tradeoffs

SECTION 4 — Implementation Roadmap
- Phases (Phase 1, Phase 2…)
- Tasks for each phase
- Milestones

SECTION 5 — Testing Strategy
- What to test
- Edge cases
- Manual test checklist

SECTION 6 — Rollout & Lifecycle
- Deployment notes
- Feature flag (if applicable)
- Follow-up iterations

Formatting:
- Prefer bullets
- Keep concise
- Avoid speculation beyond our discussion

3️⃣ Lifecycle management:
- FEATURE_PLAN.md is the **active working plan**.
- When the feature is completed:
  - Summarize outcomes into docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md
  - Provide developer instructions, guidelines, and rules that should not be broken for this feature.
  - Delete docs/FEATURE_PLAN.md
  - Confirm completion.

Confirm with:
“Feature plan created/updated in docs/FEATURE_PLAN.md.”
